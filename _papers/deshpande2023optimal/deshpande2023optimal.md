# Optimal Annual Delivery Program for Liquefied Natural Gas Suppliers: A Mathematical Programming Model Considering Customer Inventory and Berth Management
## Abstract
> Natural gas (NG) is liquefied, stored, and transported in the form of liquefied natural gas (LNG) to economies worldwide. Conventionally, a supplier delivers LNG to customers via long-term contracts. However, the share of spot trading in the total LNG trade has also increased in recent years. Therefore, delivering LNG cargos to customers has become even more challenging for suppliers. Each supplier develops an annual delivery program (ADP) to plan deliveries for the upcoming year. The ADP planning problems reported in the literature neglect customer inventory and berth management, customer demand satisfaction, and the delivery of multiple LNG grades. In this paper, we incorporate these features into the ADP planning problem. A mathematical programming model is presented that determines the ADP that maximizes the supplier’s profit. The proposed formulation is demonstrated by using an illustrative example and two case studies. The developed mathematical programming model generates a near-optimal ADP in each case that satisfies each customer’s demand with an oversupply of less than 1%. These results clearly highlight the need to consider customer inventory and berth management to prevent practically infeasible solutions.
