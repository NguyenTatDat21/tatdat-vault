## Key Takeaways

Clautiaux, Sadykov, Vanderbeck & Viaud (2018) - not Alves/Cote - is the four-stage two-dimensional guillotine-cut bounded knapsack hypergraph-flow paper: forward-labelling DP, dominance, Lagrangian reduced-cost hyperarc filtering, k-stage/exact/trimmed/restricted definitions, C-2KP-RE-4,r real-world variant; the paper's own preprocessing enumerates last-stage 1D knapsack solutions for the restricted-exact case (w_max = max w_i, positions h = h_i), Valerio de Carvalho symmetry removal, Christofides-Whitlock plate reduction, smoothing by vertex contraction. Furini's MIP variables represent cuts of a plate into two subplates, same meaning as hyperarc variables x_a. Chen, Zuev & Berdikov (2010): RBLW provably dominates LW for Gaussian samples, OAS approximates the oracle in closed form and is superior for small samples, results with p=100 n=6-30 in 5000 repeats, Capon beamforming improved. Bertsimas et al. (2022) entropic robust counterpart guarantees bootstrap performance for estimate-first-optimize-later with Nadaraya-Watson/k-NN learning.
