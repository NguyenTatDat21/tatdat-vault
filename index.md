## Concepts

- [[concepts/adaptive-large-neighborhood-search-for-mip]] — ALNS-MIP adaptively chooses and budgets MIP neighborhood subproblems to improve incumbents within branch-and-bound. ( #mip #alns #primal-heuristics #optimization)
- [[concepts/annual-lng-delivery-program]] — Tactical LNG planning problem that coordinates inventory, vessel schedules, contract obligations, and optional spot-market actions over annual horizons. ( #maritime #lng #inventory-routing #tactical-planning)
- [[concepts/bilevel-vrpd-task-assignment-routing]] — A two-level optimization pattern that separates customer-to-mode assignment from route/schedule construction in VRPD, coupling global assignment search with ALNS route refinement. ( #vrp #drones #decomposition #heuristic-search)
- [[concepts/cash-based-operating-profitability]] — Profitability signal based on cash-flow components rather than accrual-adjusted earnings, shown to dominate accrual-inclusive profitability in cross-sectional return tests. ( #asset-pricing #profitability #accruals #factor-investing)
- [[concepts/cp-sat-hint-start]] — A warm-start pattern where a truck-only VRP solution is injected as CP-SAT hints, improving bound quality and final solutions in collaborative drone routing models. ( #constraint-programming #cp-sat #vrp #warm-start)
- [[concepts/drone-speed-selection-in-routing]] — Treats drone speed as an explicit optimization variable, balancing delivery speed against energy range and synchronization costs in truck-drone routes. ( #drones #optimization #energy #routing)
- [[concepts/exact-multitrip-vrptw-framework]] — A structure-based exact framework for capacitated multitrip VRPs with time windows that combines lower bounds and branch-and-cut to scale better than earlier exact approaches. ( #vehicle-routing #exact-methods #time-windows #branch-and-cut)
- [[concepts/green-vehicle-routing-problem-taxonomy]] — Structured map of Green-VRP variants by vehicle technology, objective type, operational scenario, and solver family for comparing environmental-routing models. ( #green-logistics #vehicle-routing #taxonomy #sustainability)
- [[concepts/hyper-heuristics]] — Hyper-heuristics optimize over heuristics rather than direct solutions, mainly via heuristic selection or generation with online, offline, or no learning. ( #metaheuristics #hyper-heuristics #optimization)
- [[concepts/k-multi-visit-drone-routing-problem]] — k-MVDRP models a truck with k reusable drones under payload-dependent energy limits and operation-level synchronization, optimized for minimum completion time. ( #vrp #drones #last-mile #operations-research)
- [[concepts/large-neighborhood-search]] — LNS improves solutions by repeatedly destroying and repairing large parts of a solution; ALNS adapts operator choice based on observed performance. ( #metaheuristics #lns #alns #routing)
- [[concepts/maritime-routing-under-uncertainty]] — A planning-level view of uncertainty in maritime routing and scheduling, emphasizing demand/travel-time/fuel volatility and practical mitigation patterns in industry. ( #maritime #uncertainty #routing #planning)
- [[concepts/min-cost-parallel-drone-scheduling-vrp]] — PDSVRP minimizes total truck+drone operating cost with multiple trucks and parallel drones under capacity and time constraints. ( #vrp #drones #cost-optimization #scheduling)
- [[concepts/min-cost-tsp-with-drone]] — A TSP-D variant that optimizes transportation and synchronization waiting costs, exposing trade-offs between route cost quality, delivery time, and drone usage frequency. ( #tsp #drones #objective-design #operations-research)
- [[concepts/multiple-flying-sidekicks-traveling-salesman-problem]] — mFSTSP models one truck coordinated with multiple UAVs, with launch/retrieval synchronization and payload-dependent endurance, minimizing overall completion time. ( #truck-drone-routing #synchronization #makespan #heuristics)
- [[concepts/nonlinear-drone-energy-function-in-routing]] — Models payload-aware convex drone energy and extends routing formulations to arbitrary energy functions while preserving tractable optimization structure. ( #drone-routing #energy-modeling #optimization #nonlinear)
- [[concepts/ordering-information-portfolio-selection]] — Portfolio-construction paradigm that uses ranking beliefs about expected returns, rather than precise return forecasts, while retaining explicit risk controls. ( #portfolio-optimization #decision-theory #risk #preference-modeling)
- [[concepts/parallel-metaheuristics-cooperative-search]] — Parallel metaheuristics run multiple search processes; cooperative variants exchange information to improve robustness and solution quality over independent runs. ( #metaheuristics #parallel-computing #optimization)
- [[concepts/payload-induced-drone-battery-consumption-rate]] — Battery draw grows with payload in drone delivery, and this dependency should be modeled explicitly in feasibility and scheduling decisions. ( #drones #energy-modeling #scheduling #operations-research)
- [[concepts/pdsvrp-c]] — PDSVRP-c extends truck-and-drone routing to multiple trucks plus collaborative drone missions, with a min-makespan objective and synchronization constraints. ( #vrp #drones #last-mile #operations-research)
- [[concepts/periodic-scheduling-under-capacity-constraints]] — Family of periodic service-allocation models with per-slot capacity limits, balancing fairness or cost via exact tree constructions and approximation guarantees. ( #periodic-scheduling #operations-research #approximation-algorithms #fairness)
- [[concepts/set-orienteering-problem]] — SOP maximizes cluster-level profit under a route-duration budget, with both matheuristic and branch-and-cut exact strategies now defining the practical solution landscape. ( #orienteering #combinatorial-optimization #routing #profits)
- [[concepts/set-team-orienteering-problem-with-time-windows]] — STOPTW extends SOP/TOPTW by assigning profits to customer clusters while enforcing customer and depot time windows over multiple tours. ( #orienteering #time-windows #clustered-routing #heuristics)
- [[concepts/tabu-search]] — Tabu Search is a memory-augmented local search method that allows non-improving moves while using tabu tenure and aspiration to escape local optima. ( #metaheuristics #local-search #operations-research)
- [[concepts/variable-neighborhood-search]] — VNS systematically changes neighborhood structures during search; key variants include VND, RVNS, and BVNS with shake-and-descent cycles. ( #metaheuristics #local-search #neighborhood-search)
- [[concepts/vehicle-routing-with-synchronization-constraints]] — VRPMS couples route design with temporal synchronization windows between service layers, requiring specialized repair and feasibility handling. ( #vrp #synchronization #constraint-programming #alns)
